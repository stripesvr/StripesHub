## Using the site

- **Click to Enter** starts the music. The track keeps playing as you move between pages.
- **Search:** On pages with a search box, press `/` to jump to it. Press `Esc` to clear it.
- **Copy buttons** on commands copy them straight to your clipboard.
- **Hover** over buttons and cards to see them tilt toward your cursor.
